Dynamic's `/shop` could not be fetched; `/store` had template placeholders. Shoe Shelter also contained inconsistent placeholder textile descriptions and conflicting color lists. Those details were excluded. No supplied payment screenshot was available.

No reuse permission for reference images, branding or prose was established. The implementation therefore uses original SVG sample illustrations and freshly written sample copy. No competitor prices, availability or warranty claims were imported into Rufus products. Source URLs above document research; `products.sourceUrls` records sources for any future authorized import. Current sample products have no imported facts and therefore use empty source arrays.

Fixtures deliberately include storage/racks plus a clearly marked footwear example to exercise both attribute models. All fixture dimensions, price, original price, available stock and materials are labeled sample data. Their warranty approval flag is false. Do not seed these fixtures into production.
